The Light Test 💡

By Lens 🔎 — a pocket check for card stock, and one of the oldest tells in the binder.

How to do it

Hold the card in front of a bright light or your phone flashlight and take the photo. Real card stock has a dark core and blocks the light — a dull glow at most. Thin fake stock glows translucent.

In the checker, this photo is an optional extra after your front and back photos of a Pokémon card — take it when you can, skip it when you can't. A slab is sealed in its case and a plush has no card stock, so the light test belongs to loose cards.

What you're looking at

A dull glow at most: real card stock is built around a dark core layer, so bright light barely gets through. The card stays mostly dark — that is the stock doing its job.

Glowing translucent: thin stock with no dark core lets the light pour through, and the art shows through like a lampshade. That is a tell worth writing down — one tell, on its own.

Its honest limits — by Lens 🔎

The light test reads the stock, not the art. A card can pass it and still be a reprint, or fail it and need a closer look. It's evidence, not a verdict.

One tell among many

The checkers never sign a verdict on one tell — the print, the lettering, the shine, and the stock all get read together, front AND back. The light test speaks only for the stock, so it rides along as evidence beside the rest: never a standalone verdict input, and never a pass/fail gate. In a check, skipping it never blocks anything — the front and back photos are the ones a verdict needs.

Card Condition Grader 🩹

Its own window, its own job: scan a photo of your card — front and back — and get its condition graded: corners, edges, surface, and centering, on the standard scale from Mint down to Damaged.

Condition is not real-or-fake

This grader reads wear only — it never says whether a card is genuine. A counterfeit can be mint and a genuine card can be beat up. For real-or-fake, that's the Checker's window, and it stays separate from this one.

5 free condition grades to start

Grade a card

Lay the card flat, keep glare off it, and fill the frame — all four corners and edges in view on both sides, because that's exactly what gets graded. Out of a sleeve if you can; sleeves hide surface wear.

The rules, in plain words 🕯️

By Wick 🕯️ · updated Oct 23, 2026

  • You use a free check only when the answer is ready. Never for a retake. Never for Inconclusive.
  • Two checkers sign before you see an answer. One is not enough.
  • Each answer is an opinion from your photos — Likely Genuine, Likely Counterfeit, or Inconclusive. How sure we are is shown as five bubbles. Never as a percent.
  • We show a card's price only when real eBay sales back it up. We always say where it came from, when, and how many sales it uses.
  • Unlimited costs $4.99/month. The limit is 300 checks a month. The Condition Pass costs $2.99/month and covers condition grades only.
  • Grades are shown just as the grading company shows them. We never change a grade.
