// 07 — THE CORRECTIONS LOG

We fix our mistakes in public

Wrong finish, wrong set, wrong number — when we catch one (or a buyer does), the listing gets fixed and the correction gets logged here, newest first. No quiet edits. Spot one we missed? Message us on eBay and it lands on this page.

Oct 212026

Ascended Heroes dropdown labels re-numbered

The card numbers in our Ascended Heroes dropdown listings were corrected against the numbers printed on the physical cards. If a dropdown ever disagrees with the card in the photo, the card is right — and now the labels match it.

Oct 212026

Eelektrik 060/217 — finish corrected to Regular

It had been listed with the wrong finish. The finish in the title is the finish you get — that's the rule this one broke, so it's fixed and logged.

Oct 212026

“Cosmic Eclipse” singles re-titled to their true sets

A batch of singles carrying Cosmic Eclipse titles turned out to belong to other sets — Unified Minds and Vivid Voltage among them. Every one re-titled to the set actually printed on the card.

Earlier corrections — Oct 19 and before — TAP TO OPEN ▾
Oct 192026

The Meowth mistitle

A Team Rocket's Meowth got titled off a database entry instead of the card in hand. The physical card disagreed — the physical card won. Titles now come from the card first, databases second.

Oct 182026

Lost Mewtwo — full refund

A buyer's Base Set Mewtwo Holo never arrived in the mail. Full $20.78 refund issued — our problem or the post office's, the buyer doesn't pay for it.

Oct 52026

Two “holo” Charizards pulled

Two XY Evolutions Charizard 11/108 copies showed no holo at any angle — and that card only exists as a holo rare. They were fakes out of a trade lot: pulled from inventory, never sold. See Fake of the Week below.

Oct 42026

Seven fake GX cards caught in a trade lot

A 127-card trade came in with seven counterfeits hiding in it. The light test and in-hand checks caught every one; they were purged from inventory that same night, before a single one could be listed.
