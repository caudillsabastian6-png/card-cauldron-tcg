Delete Your Data

You can ask Card Cauldron TCG (including the Real or Replica app) to delete some or all of the data tied to your account — without deleting the account itself.

How to request it

Send an email to caudillsabastian6@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line Delete my data, and tell us what you want removed — for example, a single submitted check and its photos, all of your submitted checks, or everything we hold about you short of the account itself. Sending the request from your account's own email address is how we verify the data is yours.

What can be deleted this way

The card photos you submitted for checks, your check history and binder records, and any city and state you chose to share in the app. If what you are asking for amounts to the whole account, we will treat it as an account deletion instead — see Delete Your Account.

How long it takes

We complete verified requests within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done. If a law requires us to keep a specific record, we keep only that record, only for as long as the law requires, and it is not used for anything else.
