Delete Your Account

This page covers accounts for Card Cauldron TCG apps, including Real or Replica.

How to request deletion

Send an email to caudillsabastian6@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line Delete my account. Sending the request from your account's own email address is how we verify the account is yours. There is no fee, and you do not need to give a reason.

What gets deleted

When your request is verified, we delete your account and the data tied to it: your account email address and sign-in information, your name and profile picture if you signed in with Google, the card photos you submitted for checks, your check history and binder records, and any city and state you chose to share in the app.

How long it takes

We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done. If a law requires us to keep a specific record, we keep only that record, only for as long as the law requires, and it is not used for anything else.

Deleting only some of your data

If you want particular data removed — for example, one submitted check and its photos — without deleting your whole account, see Delete Your Data.
